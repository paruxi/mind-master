How to play

  1. Crack the secret code: 4 colors from red, blue, green, yellow, purple and orange.
  2. Click a slot in the lit row, then click a color. Undo clears the last color.
  3. Press "Check guess" to get clues in the small pegs.
  4. Black peg: right color, right spot. White peg: right color, wrong spot.
  5. Colors can repeat. You have 10 attempts.
